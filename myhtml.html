Set in a waterlogged Tokyo experiencing non-stop rain, 16-year-old Hodaka Morishima runs away from his home on a remote island. He struggles to survive in the big city until he finds work at a small supernatural writing agency. He soon meets Hina Amano, a kind girl who can temporarily stop the rain and bring out the sun just by praying. Realizing the potential of this gift, Hodaka and Hina start a business where they bring clear skies for special events. However, Hina's power comes with a heavy personal cost: she is a weather maiden whose use of this ability will cause her to eventually vanish into the sky to restore natural balance. When Hina sacrifices herself and the blue skies return, a heartbroken Hodaka chooses to rescue her, letting Tokyo remain flooded rather than losing the person he loves.
